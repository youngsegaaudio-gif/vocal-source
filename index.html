About

A free idea list

VOCAL SOURCE is a catalog of song titles, artists, years, and short notes about how a vocal might be chopped into hardstyle. It does not host audio or lyrics.

You source the recording yourself. You clear the chop. Search links are a starting point, not a download.

Ads can come later. Tonight this is just the site.
